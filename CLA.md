## How to sign

Signing is handled automatically on GitHub. When you open your first pull
request against [secdev/scapy](https://github.com/secdev/scapy), a bot will
comment asking you to sign. To sign, post a **new comment on that pull
request** whose entire body is exactly:

```text
I have read the Scapy CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your GitHub username and the time of signing are then recorded in
`signatures/v1/cla.json` on the `cla-signatures` branch of the repository. You
only need to do this once; later pull requests are checked against that record.

If the check does not update after you sign, comment `recheck` on the pull
request.

If you are contributing on behalf of a company and your employer requires a
Corporate CLA, please open an issue so the maintainers can arrange one.
